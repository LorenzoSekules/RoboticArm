1. Installation

The required MATLAB configuration for the SDTLIB_v1_10_R2020b:
  • MATLAB R2020b or later,
  • control_toolbox
  • robust_toolbox
  • simulink
  • simulink_control_design
  • symbolic_toolbox
Once the folder SDTLIB_V1_10_R2020b folder is unzipped on your computer at: yourDirectory>SDTLIB_V1_10_R2020b, you can add it to MATLAB's path and navigate to your working directory and open the SDTlib using the SIMILINK library browser or typing SDTlibV1:
SDTlibV1
The SDTlibV1 is a SIMULINK library, depicted in the following Figure and is composed of 5 sub-libraries:
  • 6 dof bodies,
  • 6 dof mechanisms,
  • 3/6 dof DCM,
  • Predefined subsystems,
  • Space subsystems (only with the full license of the SDTlib).
This library can be used like any SIMULINK libraries: you can select and drag to your current SIMULINK model any blocks available in the sublibraries.

2. Tutorials

To better use the SDTlib, it is recommanded to read and play the various tutorials available in the subfolders: Tutorial1, Tutorial2, ...
Each tutorial is composed of a live-script tutorial1.mlx, tutorial2.mlx, ... and one or several SIMULINK models. Only a few tutorials are available with the academic license of the SDTlib (see the following list). All the tutorials are available with the full licence Nevertheless, the HTML files exported from the live-scripts are provided whatever the license.
  • Tutorial1: introduction (academic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial2: a spacecraft with 2 symmetrical rotating solar arrays (acdemic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial3: robust attitude control design of a spacecraft with 2 symmetrical rotating solar arrays (academic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial4: a spacecraft with 2 symmetrical rotating solar arrays defined by a NASTRAN model (full license),
  • Tutorial5: a short course on TITOP (Two-Input Two-Output Ports) models (academic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial6: a 6 dofs robotic arm on a chaser spacecraft (full license),
  • Tutorial7: the cantilevered plate dynamics studied with SDTlib block: NINOP Nastran body (full license),
  • Tutorial8: a spacecraft with 2 symmetrical rotating solar arrays defined by a generic plate model (academic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial9: a solar array with 3 pannels (full license),
  • Tutorial10: on-board angular momentum (academic and full licenses),
  • Tutorial11: the 4 bar mechanism (full license),
  • Tutorial12: simulating a SDTlib-based model (full license),
  • Tutorial13: the crank-slider mechanism (full license),
  • Tutorial14: kinematic and dynamic models and control of a planar 3 d.o.fs flexible arm (academic and full licenses).
