Multi port rigid body

Multi-port rigid body linear model.

It computes the NINOP model of a rigid body $\mathcal{B}$ with:

  • $n$ ports composed of $n-1$ child ports and $1$ parent port. Indeed the motion for a rigid can only be prescribed at a single parent port,
  • in its reference frame $\mathcal{R}_b$.

It results in a $[18(n-1)+6]\times [6(n-1)+18]$ dynamic model between:

  • the wrenches applied at the $n-1$ child ports and the motion vector at the single parent port (inputs),
  • the motion vectors at the $n-1$ child ports and the wrench applied by the rigid body at the parent port (output).
The single parent port can be any of the $n$ ports.

See also: General notations, Multi port rigid free body, massless connection body, 1-port NASTRAN flexible body.

Description

The Newton- Euler equations applied to a rigid body $\mathcal{B}$ at its center of mass $B$ reads:

$$ \underbrace{\left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{F}_{ext/\mathcal B} \\ \mathbf{T}_{ext/\mathcal B,B} \end{array}\right]}_{\mathbf W_{ext/\mathcal B,B}} = \underbrace{\left[\begin{array}{cc} m^{\mathcal{B}} \mathbf{1}_3 & \mathbf{0}_{3 \times 3} \\ \mathbf{0}_{3 \times 3} & \mathbf{I}^{\mathcal{B}}_B \end{array}\right]}_{\mathbf D_B^{\mathcal{B}}} \underbrace{\left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{a}^{\mathcal B}_{B} \\ \boldsymbol{\dot\omega}^{\mathcal B} \end{array}\right]}_{\mathbf{x''}^{\mathcal B}_B} + \left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{0}_{3 \times 1} \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}^{\mathcal B} \times \mathbf{I}^{\mathcal{B}}_B \boldsymbol{\omega}^{\mathcal B} \end{array}\right] $$

The linearized Newton-Euler equations assume that $\boldsymbol{\omega}^{\mathcal B}$ is small. The quadratic terms can be neglected, leading to these equations :

$$ \mathbf{W}_{ext/\mathcal B,B} = \mathbf D_B^{\mathcal{B}}\mathbf{x''}^\mathcal B_B. $$

Considering the various kinematics models $\boldsymbol\tau_{PiPj}$ where $P_j$ is the single parent port (see Kinematic model on the motion vector):

$$ \Sigma_{i=1,\cdots n;\,(i\neq j)}\boldsymbol\tau_{PiPj}^T \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal B,P_i} + \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal B,P_j} = \underbrace{\boldsymbol\tau_{BP_j}^T\mathbf D_B^{\mathcal{B}}\boldsymbol\tau_{BP_j}}_{\mathbf D_{P_j}^{\mathcal{B}}}\mathbf{x''}^\mathcal B_{P_j} . $$

Then, the NINOP model of the rigid body $\mathcal{B}$ in its reference frame $\mathcal{R}_b$ at different points $P_i$ with $i=\{1,... n\}$ can be obtain following this scheme (in this example the single parent port is port # 1):

Ports

Inputs:

  • for the childs port $i\neq j$ (the single parent port): W./body,Pi: the wrenches (units: $N$ and $N.m$) applied to the rigid body $\mathcal{B}$ at the different points $P_i$ in the body frame $\mathcal{R}_b$: $ \left[\mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{B},P_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_b} $.
  • for the single parent port $j$: m_Pj: the linearized motion vector (units: $m/s^2$, $rd/s^2$, $m/s$, $rd/s$, $m$, $rd$) at the point $P_j$ in the body frame $\mathcal{R}_b$: $\left[\delta\mathbf{m}^{\mathcal B}_{P_j} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_b}$.

Outputs:

  • for the childs port $i\neq j$ (the single parent port): m_Pi: the linearized motion vectors (units: $m/s^2$, $rd/s^2$, $m/s$, $rd/s$, $m$, $rd$) at the different points $P_i$ in the body frame $\mathcal{R}_b$: $\left[\delta\mathbf{m}^{\mathcal B}_{P_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_b}$.
  • for the single parent port $j$:Wbody/.,Pj: the wrench (units: $N$ and $N.m$) applied by the rigid body $\mathcal{B}$ at the point $P_j$ in the body frame $\mathcal{R}_b$: $ \left[\mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{B}/.,P_j} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_b} $.

Parameters

All parameters are expressed in the body frame $\mathcal{R}_b$. Each parameter can be an uncertain parameter declared with "ureal".

The main parameters are:

  • the body mass (unit: $kg$)
  • the $3 \times 3$ inertia matrix of the body expressed at its center of mass $G$ (unit of each term: $kg.m^2$)
  • the $3\times 1$ vector $\overrightarrow{OG}$: the position of the center of mass $G$ in $\mathcal{R}_b$ (unit: $m$)
  • the number of ports (more exactly, the number $n$ of points $P_i$ with $i=\{1,... n\}$ where the model has to be computed)
  • the number of the single parent port (between $1$ and $n$)
  • the $3\times 1$ vectors $\overrightarrow{OP_i}$, $i=\{1,... n\}$: the positions of the connection points $P_i$ in $\mathcal{R}_b$ (unit: $m$).
