multi-port flexible plate

Multi-port, linear and analytical model of a flexible plate.

This block implements the $(18n_C+6n_P) \times (6n_C+18n_P)$ multi-port model of a flexible plate where $n_C$ is the number or child modes $C_i$, $i=1,\cdots,n_C$ (points of the plate connected to a child sub-structure(s)) and $n_P$ is the number of parent nodes $P_i$, $i=1,\cdots,n_P$ (points of the plate connected to a parent sub-structure(s)). To each of the nodes $C_i$ or $P_i$, corresponds a $18\times 6$ or a $6\times 18$ channel:

  • at port $C_i$, $18\times 6$ channel is the transfer from the wrench applied to the plate at $C_i$ by the child substructure to the motion vector of $C_i$,
  • at port $P_i$, the $6\times 18$ channel is the transfer from the motion vector of $P_i$ to the wrench applied by the plate to the parent substructure at $P_i$.

The obtained model is expressed in its reference frame $\mathcal{R}_a $.

If $n_P=0$, then the model is $18n_C\times 6n_C$ and includes $12$ integrators to model the $6$ rigid modes at the point $C_{n_C}$ (the last port).

See also: General notations, 1-port flexible body, 2 port flexible beam, N-port NASTRAN flexible body, multi-port FEM Kirchhoff plate, Multi-Port_rigid_body

Description

Considering a plate $\mathcal{A}$ described in the following Figure

and characterized by

  • the local frame $\mathcal{R}_a$,
  • the dimensions: $l_x$, $l_y$, $h$,
  • mass density: $\rho$,
  • Young modulus $E$ and Poisson coefficient $\nu$.

Let us define:

  • $\left[\mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C_i}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: the external wrench ($6\times 1$) applied to the beam at point $C_i$, expressed in $\mathcal{R}_a$,
  • $\left[\delta\mathbf m^{\mathcal A}_{C_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: the motion vector ($18\times 1$) of the beam at point $C_i$, expressed in $\mathcal{R}_a$,
  • $\left[\delta\mathbf m^{\mathcal A}_{P_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: the motion vector ($18\times 1$) of the beam at point $P_i$, expressed in $\mathcal{R}_a$,
  • $\left[\mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,P_i}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: the wrench ($6\times 1$) applied by the beam at point $P_i$, expressed in $\mathcal{R}_a$.
For each port, the 6 degrees-of-freedom ($\mbox{trans}_{\;x}$, $\mbox{trans}_{\;y}$, $\mbox{trans}_{\;z}$, $\mbox{rot}_{\;x}$, $\mbox{rot}_{\;y}$, $\mbox{rot}_{\;z}$) are split into:
  • [$\mbox{trans}_{\;x},\;\mbox{trans}_{\;y},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;z}$] for which the motion is assumed to be rigid (see also: Rigid dynamics)
  • [$\mbox{trans}_{\;z},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;x},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;y}$] for which the motion is flexible (see also: Flexible dynamics) and computed using a $5$-th order polynomial approximation in $x$ and $y$ of the deflection $w(x,y,t)$ (see also: Finite element based N-Port model for preliminary design of multibody systems (F. Sanfedino et al.)).

Then, inversion channel function (see also: invio) is used to switch from child node to parent node.

Ports

Inputs:

  • W./body,Ci $=\left[\mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C_i}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: 6 component signal (units: $N$ and $N.m$) for $i=1,\cdots,n_C$,
  • m_Pi $=\left[\delta\mathbf m^{\mathcal A}_{P_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: 18 component signal (units: $m/s^2$, $rd/s^2$, $m/s$, $rd/s$, $m$, $rd$).

Outputs:

  • m_Ci $=\left[\delta\mathbf m^{\mathcal A}_{C_i} \right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: 18 component signal (units: $m/s^2$, $rd/s^2$, $m/s$, $rd/s$, $m$, $rd$),
  • Wbody/.,Pi $=\left[\mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,P_i}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}$: 6 component signal (units: $N$ and $N.m$) for $i=1,\cdots,n_P$.

Parameters

The parameters are:

  • length along $x$ (unit: $m$): $l_x$,
  • width along $y$ (unit: $m$): $l_y$,
  • thickness along $z$ (unit: $m$): $h$,
  • mass density (unit: $Kg/m^3$): $\rho$,
  • Young modulus (unit: $N/m^2$): $E$,
  • Poisson coefficient; $\nu$,
  • damping ratio; $\xi$,
  • number of child nodes (integer): $n_C$,
  • number of parent nodes (integer): $n_P$,
  • position of each child node (unit: $m$): $[x_{C_i},\;y_{C_i},\;0]^T\quad i=1,\cdots, n_C$,
  • position of each parent node (unit: $m$): $[x_{P_i},\;y_{P_i},\;0]^T\quad i=1,\cdots, n_P$,
  • percentage of uncertainty of the first $N_f$ cantilevered frequencies at parent node $P_1$,
  • number of uncertain cantilevered frequencies ($N_f$).

Rigid dynamics:

Rigid dynamics concerns the 3 degrees-of-freedom $\mbox{trans}_{\;x},\;\mbox{trans}_{\;y},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;z}$ of the plate.

At the first step all the $n=n_C+n_P$ nodes (child or parent) are considered as child nodes, and the $6n \times 6n$ inverse dynamics model $[\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C},P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^{-1}$ of the plate between the $n$ wrenches applied at all the nodes and the $n$ acceleration twists at the theses nodes is computed using the block multi-port rigid body. $$ \left[\begin{array}{c} \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{a}_{C_1}\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{C_1} \end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{a}_{C_{n_C}}\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{C_{n_C}} \end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{a}_{P_1}\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{P_1} \end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{a}_{P_{n_C}}\\ \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{P_{n_P}} \end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \end{array}\right] = \underbrace{ \left[\begin{array}{c} [\boldsymbol{\tau}_{C_1G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ [\boldsymbol{\tau}_{C_{n_C}G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ [\boldsymbol{\tau}_{P_1G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ [\boldsymbol{\tau}_{P_{n_P}G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \end{array}\right] [\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_G]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^{-1} \left[[\boldsymbol{\tau}_{C_1G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^T\;\cdots\; [\boldsymbol{\tau}_{C_{n_C}G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^T\;[\boldsymbol{\tau}_{P_1G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^T\;\cdots\;[\boldsymbol{\tau}_{P_{n_P}G}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}\right] }_{[\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C},P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}]_{\mathcal{R}_a}^{-1}} \left[\begin{array}{c} \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{F}_{./\mathcal{A},C_1} \\ \mathbf{T}_{./\mathcal{A},C_1}\end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{F}_{./\mathcal{A},C_{n_C}} \\ \mathbf{T}_{./\mathcal{A},C_{n_C}}\end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{F}_{./\mathcal{A},P_1} \\ \mathbf{T}_{./\mathcal{A},P_1}\end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \\ \vdots \\ \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{F}_{./\mathcal{A},P_{n_p}} \\ \mathbf{T}_{./\mathcal{A},P_{n_P}}\end{array}\right]_{\mathcal{R}_a}\end{array}\right] $$ where:

  • $G$ is the center of mass of the plate: $OG=[l_x/2\quad l_y/2\quad 0]^T_{\mathcal{R}_a}$,
  • $\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_G$ is the dynamic model of the rigid plate $\mathcal{A}$ at its center of mass $G$: $$[\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_G]_{\mathcal{R}_a}=\left[\begin{array}{cc} m^{\mathcal{A}}\mathbf{1}_3 & \mathbf{0}_{3\times 3}\\ \mathbf{0}_{3\times 3} & [\mathbf{J}^{\mathcal{A}}_G]_{\mathcal{R}_a} \end{array}\right]\quad \mbox{with: } m^{\mathcal{A}}=\rho\,l_x\,l_y\,h\quad \mbox{and } [\mathbf{J}^{\mathcal{A}}_G]_{\mathcal{R}_a}=m^{\mathcal{A}}\mbox{diag}\left(\frac{l_y^2}{12},\frac{l_x^2}{12},\frac{l_x^2+l_y^2}{12}\right)$$
  • $\boldsymbol{\tau}_{AB}$ is the kinematic model between points $A$ and $B$ .

The model $\mathbf{R}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}$ of the rigid plate between the $n_C$ child nodes and the $n_P$ parent mode is obtained from $[\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C},P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}]^{-1}$ by:

  • inverting the 6 channels of the last port relative to the node $P_{n_P}$ (see also: invio),
  • changing the sign on the 6 outputs of the channels relative to $P_{n_P}$.
  • multiplying by $0$ all the inputs and outputs of the channels relative to the nodes $P_i$, $i=1,\cdots,n_P-1$.
That is: $$\mathbf{R}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}=\mbox{diag}\left(\mathbf{1}_{6n_C},\mathbf{0}_{6(n_P-1)\times 6(n_P-1) },-\mathbf{1}_{6}\right)\left[[\mathbf{D}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C},P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}]^{-1}\right]^{-1_{[6n-5:6n]}}\mbox{diag}\left(\mathbf{1}_{6n_C},\mathbf{0}_{6(n_P-1)\times 6(n_P-1) },\mathbf{1}_{6}\right)\;.$$

Remark: note that for the rigid motion of the plate $\mathcal{A}$ only the last node $P_{n_P}$ is considered as a parent node. That is justifies considering that:

  • it is impossible to prescribe 2 independent acceleration twists at 2 different points of a rigid body,
  • closed-loop kinematic chains of pure rigid bodies are not considered here.

Flexible dynamics:

Flexible dynamics concerns the 3 degrees-of-freedom $\mbox{trans}_{\;z},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;x},\;\mbox{rot}_{\;y}$ of the plate.

The deflection of a point $(x,y)$ in the plate plane along $\mathbf{z}$ is denoted $w(x,y,t)$ and the angular deflections around $\mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}$ are, respectively:

  • $\theta_x(x,y,t) = \frac{\partial w}{\partial y}$
  • $\theta_y(x,y,t) = -\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}$
Then, let us defined: $$\mathbf{q}(x,y,t)=\left[\begin{array}{c}w(x,y,t) \\ \theta_{x}(x,y,t) \\ \theta_{y}(x,y,t) \end{array}\right]$$

Coordinates and associated shape functions:

The rigid and flexible motion can be split by introducing the parent node $O$ (located at $(0, 0)$) containing the rigid vertical displacement ($w_O(t)=w(0,0,t)$) and the linearized rotation along $x$ ($\theta_{x,O}(t)=\theta_x(0,0,t)$) and $y$ ($\theta_{y,O}=\theta_y(0,0,t)$) $$ w(x,y,t) = w_O(t) + y \theta_{x,O}(t) - x \theta_{y,O}(t) + w_f(x, y, t)\;. $$ The flexible part $w_f(x, y, t)$ is approximated by a $5$-th order polynomial in $x$ and $y$: $$w_f(x,y,t) = \mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y)^T \mathbf{a}_f(t)$$ where $\mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y)$ is a vector of 18 elementary monomials: $$\mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y) = [x^2,\; xy,\; y^2,\; x^3,\; x^2y,\; xy^2,\; y^3,\; x^4,\; x^3y,\; x^2y^2,\; xy^3,\; y^4,\; x^5,\; x^4y,\; x^3y^2,\; x^2y^3,\; xy^4,\; y^5]^T $$

Analogously:

  • $\theta_{x, f}(x,y,t)= \mathbf{P}_{\theta_{x,f}}(x,y)^T \mathbf{a}_f(t)$ with $\mathbf{P}_{\theta_{x,f}}(x,y)=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y)$
  • $\theta_{y, f}(x,y,t)= \mathbf{P}_{\theta_{y,f}}(x,y)^T \mathbf{a}_f(t)$ with $\mathbf{P}_{\theta_{y,f}}(x,y)=-\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y)$
Then one can define the $18\times 3$ matrix of monomials: $$\mathbf{P}_f(x,y)=[\mathbf{P}_{w_f}(x,y)\quad \mathbf{P}_{\theta_{x,f}}(x,y) \quad \mathbf{P}_{\theta_{y,f}}(x,y)]$$

Considering the 6 points $A_i$ of coordinates $(x_{A_i},y_{A_i})$ defined in the following Figure. Then: $$\mathbf{q}_{f,A_i}(t)=\left[\begin{array}{c}w_f(x_{A_i},y_{A_i},t) \\ \theta_{x, f}(x_{A_i},y_{A_i},t) \\ \theta_{y, f}(x_{A_i},y_{A_i},t) \end{array}\right]=\mathbf{P}_f(x_{A_i},y_{A_i})^T \mathbf{a}_f(t),\;\;\forall\;i=1,\cdots,6$$

Then one can express the vector of $18$ time-dependant coefficient $\mathbf{a}_f(t)$ as a function of the vector of flexible coordinates $\mathbf{q}_f(t)=[\mathbf{q}_{f,A_1}(t)^T,\;\;\mathbf{q}_{f,A_2}(t)^T,\cdots ,\mathbf{q}_{f,A_6}(t)^T ]^T$: $$ \mathbf{a}_f(t)= \mathbf{P}^{-1}\mathbf{q}_f(t)\quad\mbox{with}\quad \mathbf{P}_{18\times 18}=[\mathbf{P}_f(x_{A_1},y_{A_1}),\cdots,\mathbf{P}_f(x_{A_6},y_{A_6})]^T\;. $$

Then, defining the vector of the 3 rigid coordinates (at point $O$): $\mathbf{q}_r(t)=[w_O(t),\; \theta_{x,O}(t),\;\theta_{y,O}(t)]^T$, one can express: $$\mathbf{q}(x,y,t)=\boldsymbol{\Phi}_r(x,y) \mathbf{q}_r(t)+ \boldsymbol{\Phi}_f(x,y) \mathbf{q}_f(t)\quad \mbox{with:}\;\boldsymbol{\Phi}_r(x,y)=\left[\begin{array}{ccc} 1 & y & -x \\ 0 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right];\;\mbox{and}\;\boldsymbol{\Phi}_f(x,y)=\mathbf{P}_f(x,y)^T\mathbf{P}^{-1}\;.$$

Mass and stiffness matrices

The kinetic energy is given by $$ \mathcal{T} = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{l_x} \int_{0}^{l_y} \dot{\mathbf{q}}^T \left[\begin{array}{ccc} \rho h & 0 & 0\\ 0 & \frac{\rho h^3}{12} & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \frac{\rho h^3}{12}\\ \end{array}\right] \dot{\mathbf{q}} dx\, dy=\frac{1}{2} \left[\begin{array}{c}\dot{\mathbf{q}}_r \\ \dot{\mathbf{q}}_f \end{array}\right]^T\left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{M}_{rr} & \mathbf{M}_{rf}\\ \mathbf{M}_{fr} & \mathbf{M}_{ff}\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}\dot{\mathbf{q}}_r \\ \dot{\mathbf{q}}_f \end{array}\right] $$ with the mass matrix: $\mathbf{M}=\left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{M}_{rr} & \mathbf{M}_{rf}\\ \mathbf{M}_{fr} & \mathbf{M}_{ff}\end{array}\right]=\int_{0}^{l_x} \int_{0}^{l_y} \left[\begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_r^T \\ \boldsymbol\Phi_f^T \end{array}\right] \left[\begin{array}{ccc} \rho h & 0 & 0\\ 0 & \frac{\rho h^3}{12} & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \frac{\rho h^3}{12}\\ \end{array}\right][ \boldsymbol{\Phi}_r \;\; \boldsymbol\Phi_f ] dx\, dy$

(Rk: $\mathbf{M}_{rf}=\mathbf{M}_{fr}^T$)

The elastic potential energy is given by: $$ \mathcal{K} = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{l_x} \int_{0}^{l_y} \mathbf{\kappa}^T \mathbf{D} \mathbf{\kappa} dx\, dy $$ The curvatures and the flexural rigidity matrix are given by $$ \mathbf{\kappa} = \left[\begin{array}{c} -\frac{\partial^2 w_f}{\partial x^2} \\ -\frac{\partial^2 w_f}{\partial y^2} \\ -2 \frac{\partial^2 w_f}{\partial x\partial y} \\ \end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{c} -\frac{\partial^2 P_{w_f}^T}{\partial x^2} \\ -\frac{\partial^2 P_{w_f}^T}{\partial y^2} \\ -2 \frac{\partial^2 P_{w_f}^T}{\partial x\partial y} \\ \end{array}\right]\mathbf{P}^{-1}\mathbf{q}_f=\mathbf\Phi_{\mathbf{\kappa}}\mathbf{q}_f \quad \mbox{and}\quad \mathbf{D}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc} D & D \nu & 0\\ D \nu & D & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \frac{D}{2} (1 - \nu) \end{array}\right] $$ where $D = \frac{E h^3}{12 (1 - \nu^2)}$ is the flexural stiffness and $\nu$ is the Poisson ratio. Thus $\mathcal{K} = \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{q}_f^T\mathbf{K}\mathbf{q}_f$ with $\mathbf{K}=\int_{0}^{l_x} \int_{0}^{l_y} \mathbf\Phi_{\mathbf{\kappa}}^T\mathbf{D}\mathbf\Phi_{\mathbf{\kappa}}dx\,dy$.

2-port model

Let us denote:

  • $\mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O}=[F_O^z, T_O^x, T_O^y]^T$ the 3 d.o.f wrench (one force and 2 torques) applied by the plate to the parent substructure at the point $O$,
  • $\mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C}=[F_C^z, T_C^x, T_C^y]^T$ the 3 d.o.f wrench (one force and 2 torques) applied to the plate at a given child node $C\,(x_C,y_C)$.
Then the work of these wrenches read: $\mathcal{W}_e(t)=-\mathbf{q}(0,0,t)^T\mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O}(t)+\mathbf{q}(x_C,y_C,t)^T\mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C}(t)$ or: $$\mathcal{W}_e= \left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{q}_r \\ \mathbf{q}_f \end{array}\right]^T\left[\begin{array}{cc}-\mathbf{1}_3 & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_r(x_C,y_C)^T \\ \mathbf{0}_{18\times 3} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_f(x_C,y_C)^T \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O} \\ \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C} \end{array}\right]$$ Thus the dynamic model reads: $$ \left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{M}_{rr} & \mathbf{M}_{rf}\\ \mathbf{M}_{fr} & \mathbf{M}_{ff}\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_r \\ \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_f \end{array}\right]+ \left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{0}_{3\times 3}& \mathbf{0}_{3\times 18}\\ \mathbf{0}_{18\times 3} & \mathbf{K} \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{q}_r \\ \mathbf{q}_f \end{array}\right]= \left[\begin{array}{cc}-\mathbf{1}_3 & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_r(x_C,y_C)^T \\ \mathbf{0}_{18\times 3} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_f(x_C,y_C)^T \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O} \\ \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C} \end{array}\right]$$

The modal coordinates are introduced by solving the eigenvalue problem $ \mathbf{M}_{ff} \mathbf{V}\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 = \mathbf{K} \mathbf{V} \qquad \mbox{where } \boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 = \mbox{diag}(\omega_i^2)$

Applying the transformation $\mathbf{q}_f = \mathbf{V} \boldsymbol{\eta}$ the system becomes: $$ \left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{M}_{rr} & \mathbf{L}_{O}^T\\ \mathbf{L}_{O} & \mathbf{1}_{18\times 18}\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_r \\ \ddot{\boldsymbol{\eta}} \end{array}\right]+ \left[\begin{array}{cc}\mathbf{0}_{3\times 3}& \mathbf{0}_{3\times 18}\\ \mathbf{0}_{18\times 3} & \boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}\mathbf{q}_r \\ \boldsymbol{\eta}\end{array}\right]= \left[\begin{array}{cc}-\mathbf{1}_3 & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_r(x_C,y_C)^T \\ \mathbf{0}_{18\times 3} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_\eta(x_C,y_C)^T \end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c} \mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O} \\ \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C} \end{array}\right]$$ where: $\mathbf{L}_{O}=\mathbf{V}^T\mathbf{M}_{fr}$ and $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_\eta=\boldsymbol{\Phi}_f \mathbf{V} $

Damping can be added by introducing matrix $\boldsymbol{\Xi}= \mbox{diag}(2 \xi_i \omega_i)$.

The 3 d.o.f. acceleration twist at child node $C$ is written as: $\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_C(t) = \ddot{\mathbf{q}}(x_C,y_C,t)=\underbrace{\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r}(x_C,y_C)}_{\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C}}\ddot{\mathbf{q}}_r(t) + \underbrace{\boldsymbol{\Phi}_\eta(x_C,y_C)}_{\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta,C}} \ddot{\boldsymbol{\eta}}(t)$

The 2 ports model ($6\times 6$ )for the plate $\mathcal{A}$ considering as inputs the acceleration twist at the parent node $O$ and the wrench at the child node $C$ is denoted $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{C,O}(\mathrm{s})$ and can be described by the following state-space representation ($\mathbf{q}_r$ is renamed $\mathbf{q}_O$): $$ \left[\begin{array}{c} \dot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \ddot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \hline \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_C \\ \mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O} \end{array}\right] = \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 18} & \mathbf{1}_{18} & \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 3} & \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 3} \\ -\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & -\boldsymbol{\Xi} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C}^T & -\mathbf{L}_O \\ \hline -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C}\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C}\boldsymbol{\Xi} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C}^T & \left(\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C} \mathbf{L}_O \right) \\ \mathbf{L}_O^T \boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & \mathbf{L}_O^T \boldsymbol{\Xi} & \left( \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C} \mathbf{L}_O\right)^T & \mathbf{L}_O^T \mathbf{L}_O - \mathbf{M}_{rr} \\ \end{array} \right] \left[\begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol{\eta} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \hline \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C}\\ \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_O \end{array}\right] $$ This model represent the case of a plate clamped at $O$ and free at $C$. Thanks to the fact that the feedthrough matrix has consists of non null terms on the diagonal the inversion of the channel is possible (see also: invio). It is therefore possible to represent other boundary conditions by inversion of one or several channels.

n-port model

Considering 2 child nodes $C_1$ and $C_2$, one can easily derived the 3-port model of the plate $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1,C_2],O}(\mathrm{s})$: $$ \left[\begin{array}{c} \dot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \ddot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \hline \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{C_1} \\ \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_{C_2} \\ \mathbf{W}_{\mathcal{A}/.,O} \end{array}\right] = \left[ \begin{array}{cc|ccc} \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 18} & \mathbf{1}_{18} & \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 3} & \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 3} & \mathbf{0}_{18 \times 3} \\ -\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & -\boldsymbol{\Xi} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1}^T & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2}^T & -\mathbf{L}_O \\ \hline -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1}\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1}\boldsymbol{\Xi} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1}^T & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2}^T & \left(\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C_1} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1} \mathbf{L}_O \right) \\ -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2}\boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & -\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2}\boldsymbol{\Xi} & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1}^T & \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2} \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2}^T & \left(\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C_2} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2} \mathbf{L}_O \right) \\ \mathbf{L}_O^T \boldsymbol{\Omega}^2 & \mathbf{L}_O^T \boldsymbol{\Xi} & \left( \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C_1} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_1} \mathbf{L}_O\right)^T & \left( \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{r,C_2} - \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\eta, C_2} \mathbf{L}_O\right)^T & \mathbf{L}_O^T \mathbf{L}_O - \mathbf{M}_{rr} \\ \end{array} \right] \left[\begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol{\eta} \\ \dot{\boldsymbol\eta} \\ \hline \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C_1}\\ \mathbf{W}_{./\mathcal{A},C_2}\\ \ddot{\mathbf{q}}_O \end{array}\right] $$

Considering now that the parent node is no more $O$ but $P\,(x_P,y_P)$, it is possible to compute the model $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{C,P}(\mathrm{s})$ by:

  • computing the model $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C,P],O}(\mathrm{s})$ considering both nodes $C$ and $P$ as child nodes
  • freeing the node $O$ (no wrench applied at $O$) and transforming the child node $P$ into a parent node by inverting the channels 4 to 9 of the model $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C,P],O}(\mathrm{s})$,
  • forgetting the $3 \times 3$ last channels (at $O$) in the model,
  • changing the sign on the 3 outputs of the channels relative to $P$.
These operations can be summarized by the mathematical expression: $$\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{C,P}(\mathrm{s})=\left[\begin{array}{cc} \mathbf{1}_3 & \mathbf{0}_{3\times 3} \\ \mathbf{0}_{3\times 3} & -\mathbf{1}_3 \end{array}\right] \left[\left[\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C,P],O}(\mathrm{s})\right]^{-1_{[4:9]}}\right]_{[1:6]}\;.$$ In this expression:
  • the notation $[\star]^{-1_{\mathbf{I}}}$ stands for the inversion in the model $\star$ of the channels listed in the vector of indices $\mathbf{I}$,
  • the notation $[\star]_\mathbf{I}$ stands for the selection in the model $\star$ of the channels listed in the vector of indices $\mathbf{I}$.

Considering the plate $\mathcal{A}$ with the $n_P$ parent nodes and $n_C$ child modes, one can easily compute the $n$-port model which size is $3n\times 3n$ ($n=n_P+n_C$): $$\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})=\left[\begin{array}{cc} \mathbf{1}_{3n_C} & \mathbf{0}_{3n_C\times 3n_P} \\ \mathbf{0}_{3n_P\times 3n_C} & -\mathbf{1}_{3n_P} \end{array}\right] \left[\left[\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C},P_1\cdots P_{n_P}],O}(\mathrm{s})\right]^{-1_{[3n_C+1:3(n+1)]}}\right]_{[1:3n]}\;.$$

Rigid/flexible dynamics assembly:

The $n$-port model $\mathbf{M\!a}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})$ (see Figure above) of the plate is finally obtained from the rigid model $\mathbf{R}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}$ by overwriting the channels numbers 3, 4 and 5 of each of the $n$ ports with the 3 channels of the corresponding port of the model $\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})$. That is: $$\mathbf{M\!a}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})=\mathbf{R}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1 \cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1 \cdots P_{n_P}]}$$ $$ [\mathbf{M\!a}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})]_{[6i+3,6i+4,6i+5]}=[\mathbf{F}^{\mathcal{A}}_{[C_1\cdots C_{n_C}],[P_1\cdots P_{n_P}]}(\mathrm{s})]_{[3i+1,3i+2,3i+3]}\;,\quad i=0,\cdots,n-1\;.$$
